# Code of Conduct

UsageMax is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

Expected behavior includes:

- being respectful and constructive;
- assuming good intent while addressing impact;
- accepting feedback and correcting mistakes;
- protecting other people's privacy and confidential information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, publishing private information, and
deliberate disruption of the project or its services.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action
when behavior violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately to
`hello@usagemax.com` with enough context to investigate. Do not include secrets
or private customer data. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical.
