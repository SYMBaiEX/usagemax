## What the CLI sends

The collector parses your local logs on your computer. It sends aggregate counts, dates, model and provider names, estimated costs, and hashed session identifiers. Prompt text, completions, source code, and provider credentials stay on the computer.

If you build a custom integration, send events containing counts and metadata. Keep prompts, outputs, headers, and secrets out of the payload.

## Who can see it

New profiles start private. Publishing a profile makes aggregate usage and activity available through its public page and leaderboard. You can change profile visibility from [your account](https://usagemax.com/account). An organization workspace keeps its private data within the organization.

## Manage data and access

Use the account page to revoke collectors, download available data, or request deletion. Revoking a key ends its write access and keeps existing totals. When a computer is reassigned, revoke its collector and remove its local link.

Read the [privacy policy](https://usagemax.com/privacy) and [security overview](https://usagemax.com/security) for policy and disclosure details.
