## Keep workspaces separate

Use the organization workspace for shared projects and your personal workspace for your own history. When linking a computer or creating a collector, check which workspace is selected before issuing the credential.

Workspace roles determine who can manage membership, collectors, allocations, billing, exports, and deletion. If a control is unavailable, contact your workspace administrator.

## Allocate usage and watch budgets

- Use **Projects** and **Cost centers** to organize usage under the teams responsible for it.
- Use **Budgets** and notifications to track spending against monthly thresholds.
- Use **Connections** for supported provider imports and review the source and status before using the results in a financial report.
- Use the **Account** controls to review devices and available privacy and retention settings.
